# Data Science Job Analysis (Yearly Trends)

## Project Overview

This project analyzes Data Science job trends over multiple years to understand how demand, salaries, job roles, and required skills have evolved over time. The goal is to extract meaningful insights from job market data and identify patterns in the data science industry.

This analysis helps in understanding industry growth and key hiring trends for data-related roles.

---

## Objective

The main objectives of this project are:

* Analyze yearly trends in Data Science job postings
* Identify growth patterns in job demand
* Explore salary trends over time
* Discover most in-demand skills and roles
* Understand industry hiring behavior

---

## Dataset Information

The dataset includes job-related information such as:

* Job Title
* Year of Posting
* Salary (if available)
* Location
* Required Skills
* Company Name
* Job Type / Role Category

---

## Key Analysis Performed

* Year-wise job demand analysis
* Trend analysis of Data Science job growth
* Salary trend comparison across years
* Most common job roles in Data Science
* Skill frequency analysis
* Location-based job distribution

* Data Science job demand shows a consistent upward trend over the years

* Salary trends show gradual growth in the field
* Python and SQL remain the most required skills
